01 / About

About Us.

Lloyds Mercantile Corporation is a pioneer in the electrical cable industry. We have an experience of more than 5 decades working with various private and government organisations. We have been supplying electrical products to various industries like steel, power, iron, mining, pipes, cement, real estate, turnkey contractors, poles, instrastructure, chemicals and many more. We deal in brands like KEI, Polycab, Rallison, Havells, Honeywell - MK, AKG and more.

03 / Clientele

Clientele.

01

Real Estate

We offer comprehensive solutions for real estate companies or contractors. Our basket of products for this segment include cables, housing wires, modular switches, circuit protection devices like MCBs, DBs, MCCBs

02

Industry

We offer a wide range of products for industrial buyers. Steel, cement, paint, iron, mining are some of the industrial segments we are catering to. Our range of products in this area include power & control cables, HT cables, instrumentation cables, heat resistant wires, rubber trailing cables, MCBs, DBs, contactors etc.

03

Infrastructure

We have been working with some of the biggest contractors in this space. We have been supplying power cables, wires, MCBs, modular switches, GI earthing wires, PVC conduits, cable managament systems to users in this segment.

04

Interior Designers

We offer complete electrical solution to interior designers who are looking for a one-stop shop for electrical procurement.

05

Government

We have been supplying to government organisations for the past several decades. Examples include BTPS, WBPDCL, PWD & CPWD contractors

06

Defense

We are a regular supplier to MES & BSF contractors and offer armoured cables, HT cables and wires.
